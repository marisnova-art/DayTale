5. How long we keep data

  • Records in the trash: deleted for good after 30 days.
  • Account deletion: when you delete your account, your records, photos and settings are deleted right away from our database and photo storage. Backups are overwritten within [BACKUP DAYS] days.
  • Inactive accounts: accounts without an active subscription are deleted one year after the last sign-in. We email you 30 days and 7 days before.
  • Accounts with an active subscription are kept while the subscription continues.
  • Payment and tax records are kept by us and by Paddle for as long as tax law requires.

6. Your rights

You can access, correct, export and delete your data at any time in the app (Settings › Data). Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act or Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act), you also have the right to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, and to complain to your data protection authority. We do not sell or share personal information as defined by California law. To use any right, email us; we answer within 30 days.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by our hosting providers. Each person’s data is separated by database access rules, and photo addresses cannot be guessed. Photos are served from long, random web addresses: anyone you give a photo’s exact address to (for example inside an exported backup) could open that one photo, so share exports with care. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

8. Children

The service is not meant for children under 14 (or the higher minimum age in your country). If we learn that a child has signed up without the required consent, we will delete the account.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we will tell you in the app or by email at least 30 days before it takes effect.
